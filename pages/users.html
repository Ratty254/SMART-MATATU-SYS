Administrator Guidelines

Important controls for protecting system accounts and access.

Account approval: Newly registered accounts remain pending until an administrator approves them.

Account suspension: Temporarily prevents a user from accessing the system without deleting their account or transaction history.

Role management: Ensure users have the appropriate permissions according to their responsibilities.

Administrator protection: Protect administrator accounts and maintain at least one active administrator.
